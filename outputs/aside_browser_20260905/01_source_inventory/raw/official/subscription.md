# Manage your Aside subscription

Use `Settings > Plan & Usage` to review your Aside plan, credits, payment method, and invoices.

## Plans

Aside has three plans.

| Plan | Includes                                                                                                       |
| ---- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free | 500 credits per month, up to 3 routines, built-in password manager, personalized memory.                       |
| Pro  | Everything in Free, 3x Free usage, Ultrabrowse, unlimited routines, and priority model access where available. |
| Max  | Everything in Pro, 30x Free usage, early access program.                                                       |

Pro and Max plans can have monthly or yearly Stripe prices when those prices are active in billing.

## Credits

Aside plans grant credits each billing period. `Settings > Plan & Usage > Credits` shows:

* remaining credit percentage
* renewal date
* monthly credit amount for the current plan

Paid-plan credits renew on the subscription's billing anniversary. A trial and its first paid period receive separate credit grants. Free-plan credits renew at the start of each UTC calendar month.

Credits are separate from API keys or external AI subscriptions you connect under [AI settings](/help/ai). If you bring your own API key, your provider may bill usage to that provider account.

## Review credit usage history

`Settings > Plan & Usage > Aside credits usage` lists credit use by task and model. Each row shows the date, task, model, and credit amount.

Use the month and year selectors to change the range. Choose `Download CSV` to export the selected month.

## Choose a plan

Choose a paid plan when you need more included credits for Aside plan models.

Choose Pro for:

* more monthly credits
* Ultrabrowse for source-heavy research
* unlimited active routines
* priority model access where available

Choose Max for the largest included credit grant and early access features.

## Change or manage billing

Open `Settings > Plan & Usage`.

From there, you can:

* choose `Adjust plan` to change your plan
* choose `Open` to manage payment details and invoices in Stripe
* review current credit usage

If you cancel a paid subscription at period end, Plan & Usage shows the cancellation date.
